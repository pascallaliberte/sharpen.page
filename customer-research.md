I interview customers to find out why they really buy, or really leave, and turn that into something a founder or product manager can act on.

Do you know someone in one of these three situations?

1.  **Churn or lost deals.** Someone who keeps losing customers or lost more than one big sale. Something isn't working, and they still don't have a real answer for why.
2.  **Overworked PM or founder.** A product person who's overwhelmed, and customer research is what keeps getting ignored. They're building and building, but they're losing touch on whether it matters.
3.  **The takeover of a product**. Anyone who just took over a product or bought a business, who's now responsible for a product they didn't build, and needs a hand to get customer ground truth more quickly.
